# Interview Prep Prompt Pack

Version: 0.7.0
Tags: pack, interview, preparation, career, technical, behavioral

Use this pack for structured interview preparation — technical, behavioral,
and system design interviews.

## Recommended Modes

* Plan
* Coach
* Teach

## Starter Prompt — Interview Strategy

```text
Use Atlas One Plan Mode.

Help me prepare for this interview.

Role: [role title]
Company: [company or type]
Interview format: [technical / behavioral / system design / mix]

Tell me:
1. What they are likely testing for at each stage
2. The three things I must communicate clearly
3. Questions I should be ready to ask them
4. My preparation steps in order
5. What to avoid
```

## Behavioral Question Prep

```text
Use Atlas One Coach Mode.

I need to prepare structured answers for behavioral interview questions.

For each situation I describe, help me build an answer using:
- Situation (brief)
- My decision or action (concrete, first person)
- Result (specific, preferably measurable)
- What I learned or would do differently

Keep answers under 2 minutes when spoken aloud.

Question: [your behavioral question]
My situation: [rough notes on what happened]
```

## Technical Concept Review

```text
Use Atlas One Teach Mode.

Explain [concept] the way I would need to explain it clearly in an interview.

Cover:
- What it is (one sentence)
- Why it exists (the problem it solves)
- How it works at a high level
- When to use it vs alternatives
- One concrete example
- Common misconceptions or traps

Then quiz me with one question I should be able to answer.
```

## System Design Prep

```text
Use Atlas One Architect Mode.

Walk me through how I should approach this system design question in an interview.

Question: [design question]

Guide me through:
1. Clarifying questions to ask before designing
2. Estimating scale and constraints
3. High-level components and their responsibilities
4. Key trade-offs to discuss
5. Where interviewers typically probe deeper
6. What a strong answer focuses on vs a weak one
```

## Example Usage

```text
Goal:
Prepare for a senior engineer interview at a product company in two days.

Mode:
Plan

Output:
What they're testing, what I must communicate, prep steps in order.
```

## When to Use

* When preparing for a specific interview with limited time
* When you need structured answers, not just a topic list
* When practicing behavioral, technical or system-design rounds

## Expected Output

* What each stage is testing for
* The few things you must communicate clearly
* Ordered preparation steps and what to avoid
* For practice rounds: a structured answer and one follow-up question

## Constraints

* Keep spoken answers realistic in length (under ~2 minutes)
* Base answers on the candidate's real experience — no fabricated stories
* Coaching only; the pack prepares you, it does not sit the interview
