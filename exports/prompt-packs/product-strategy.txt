Use this pack to shape positioning, offers, roadmaps and product direction.

---

STARTER PROMPT — paste into ChatGPT or Claude

Use Atlas One Decide Mode.

Help choose the strongest product direction.

Compare:
- audience
- problem urgency
- differentiation
- proof
- implementation cost
- risk

Recommend one direction and explain the trade-offs.

---

FOLLOW-UP: MARKET AND CONTEXT RESEARCH

Use Atlas One Research Mode.

Synthesize what is known about this product space.

Cover:
- who has this problem and how urgently
- what exists already and how it compares
- what evidence points to real demand
- what is still uncertain
- what assumptions need testing first

---

FOLLOW-UP: ROADMAP PLANNING

Use Atlas One Plan Mode.

Given the chosen direction, build a sequenced roadmap.

Include:
- goal
- assumptions
- phases with outcomes
- first three concrete tasks
- definition of done for the first milestone
- key risks and how to detect them early
