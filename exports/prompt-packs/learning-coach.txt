Use this pack to learn a subject through structured explanation and practice.

---

STARTER PROMPT — paste into ChatGPT or Claude

Use Atlas One Teach Mode.

Teach me this topic in a practical sequence.

Include:
- what I need to understand first
- a simple explanation
- one concrete example
- one practice task
- how I can tell if I understood it

---

FOLLOW-UP: CLARIFY A CONCEPT

Use Atlas One Explain Mode.

I did not fully understand this part: [paste the concept].

Give me:
- the shortest accurate answer
- a plain-language version
- one real example
- what people usually get wrong about this
- the one thing to remember

---

FOLLOW-UP: COACHING FOR PROGRESS

Use Atlas One Coach Mode.

I am trying to learn [topic] and I am stuck.

Help me:
- identify what is actually blocking me
- see what my current state is
- find one small action I can take today
- stay honest about what I do and do not know yet

End with one question that helps me think more clearly about this.
