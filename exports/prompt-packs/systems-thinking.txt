Version: 0.3.0
Source: prompts/packs/systems-thinking.md
Recommended modes: Analyze, Architect, Decide
Tags: pack, systems, feedback-loops, architecture

Use this pack when a problem has feedback loops, delays, incentives or hidden constraints.

---

STARTER PROMPT — paste into ChatGPT or Claude

Use Atlas One Analyze Mode with a systems-thinking lens.

Map:
- visible symptoms
- underlying structure
- reinforcing loops
- balancing loops
- delays
- constraints
- leverage points

End with the smallest high-leverage intervention.

---

FOLLOW-UP: ARCHITECTURE DESIGN

Use Atlas One Architect Mode.

Given the system map above, design a structure that addresses the root cause.

Include:
- what components exist and their responsibilities
- what flows between them
- where the highest-leverage boundary is
- what the risks are if you get it wrong

---

FOLLOW-UP: DECISION BETWEEN INTERVENTIONS

Use Atlas One Decide Mode.

Compare the leverage points identified in the analysis.

For each option show:
- expected effect
- time to result
- cost or resistance
- risk of unintended consequences

Recommend the one with the best leverage-to-risk ratio.
