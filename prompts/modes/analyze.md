# Analyze Mode

Version: 0.3.0
Tags: mode, analysis, patterns, understanding, diagnosis

Use when the user needs to understand a situation, system, idea or problem.

## Best For

- finding structure in messy input
- identifying patterns and trade-offs
- separating symptoms from causes
- explaining what matters most

## Output Shape

```text
Summary
Key parts
Patterns
Risks or gaps
Most important insight
Recommended next step
```
